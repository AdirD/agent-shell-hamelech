# Aggregated product signals

Period: last 60 days. These figures are synthetic test data, not real users.

- 34 paying workspaces; 21 have keyword routing enabled.
- 18,420 tickets received.
- 31% of tickets were reassigned at least once, but the event does not
  distinguish incorrect routing from workload balancing.
- Median first assignment time: 4 minutes. For the eight busiest workspaces:
  19 minutes.
- Five cancellation surveys exist. One mentions "too much manual queue cleanup."
- Nine support conversations ask for routing improvements:
  - four want rules based on customer plan or account owner
  - three want suggested priority or labels
  - two ask for "AI routing" without describing desired behavior
- Two workspaces disabled rules after unexpected assignments.
- No event currently records whether an assignment suggestion was accepted or
  corrected.

Caveats:

- Workspace size and ticket volume are heavily skewed.
- Reassignment is not a validated proxy for a wrong initial assignment.
- Support requests come from admins, while daily triage is often done by team
  leads or agents.
