# Other everyday pictures for the same idea

The plumbing question — *are we rerouting the house, or replacing the
washer?* — is the canonical form. Some people's intuition grabs a different
everyday picture faster. Pick whichever one your brain already owns; they're
all the same question in different clothes. Use one instead of the plumbing
line when it will land better for the person you're talking to, not as an
extra on top of it.

- 🚰 **Plumbing** — did we reroute the house, or replace the washer?
- 🎒 **Packing** — are we checking a suitcase for a weekend trip, or are we
  carrying that suitcase around forever now?
- 🏗️ **Renovation permits** — did fixing a squeaky door require pulling a
  permit that now applies to the whole building?
- 🔧 **Toolbox** — did we grab the one tool this job needed, or did we buy a
  whole new toolkit we now have to store, maintain, and explain to the next
  person?
- 🧵 **Alterations** — did we hem the one pair of pants, or restitch the
  whole wardrobe so that one pair fits?
